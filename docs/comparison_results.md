## Reading these numbers

Both detectors set their threshold at the 95th percentile of *benign* scores, so roughly 5% of benign traffic is flagged by construction. That fixes the false-positive budget up front and caps recall in exchange for high precision -- the intended trade-off for a triage feed, where analyst time is the scarce resource. Lowering the percentile raises recall and costs precision; the ROC-AUC column is the threshold-free comparison.
